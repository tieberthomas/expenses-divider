# Domain overview

A short walkthrough of Expenses Divider.

Expenses Divider is for a trip: people record what they paid and see who still owes whom.

Someone starts an **expenses group** (the notebook for that journey). They are the **creator**. They give the group a name, list the **members** on the trip, and get a **group URL**. Money is tracked between those names, not between accounts. A group has a currency. For now every group is in euro.

A **public group** is accessible to anyone with the URL. No account is needed. A **private group** is accessible only to someone signed in with Google whose email is listed on the group.

The creator manages who is on the trip and can change any expense.

Members add **expenses**: a bill with a description and a total. **Payments** say who paid how much (one person or several). **Cost shares** say who the bill was for. Payers and cost-share members can differ. Cost shares can be an even split or custom amounts.

Each member then has a **balance**: what they paid minus their cost shares. The Expenses Divider then provides a **settlement suggestion** which is a short list of who should pay whom so everyone is even. It is not a record that money actually moved.
