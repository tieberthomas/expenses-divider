# Product context

Expenses Divider is a web app for friends on a trip: they record who paid for what and see who still owes whom. Simpler than Splitwise for that case. Not for rent, bookkeeping, or living together long term.

Using it is as simple as sharing a **link**. No account and no app download.

Most people will use it on a **phone**, including older phones. Design **mobile first**. It must feel **fast**. The design should be super **intuitive**.

The UI is **German and English** (default English).

The first version is **euro** only. More currencies will follow (trips are often not only euro). Converting between currencies is not planned.

Not now: native app, custom domain, sending emails
